## Open a project

Point Ubiq at a folder on disk and it becomes a project: a name, a home folder, and the rail modes
that work against it. Ubiq remembers the projects you have opened, so the next launch offers them
back rather than asking you to browse again.

## Start an agent

With a project open, use the **+** control to start an agent. Pick which harness — Claude Code,
Codex, Gemini CLI, opencode or Copilot CLI — and Ubiq spawns it in a real terminal [pane](panes),
under [Agents mode](agents-starting). Type into the pane exactly as you would in a terminal window;
Ubiq adds nothing between your keystrokes and the harness.

## Find your way around

The rail on the left switches what the centre of the window is for — [IDE, Git, Agents, the
knowledge base and the rest](workbench-modes). Panels dock, move and close like the rest of the
window; see [Panels and the dock](workbench-panels).
