Use this when the window is hard to read or you want a different look.

1. To flip between dark and light, press the theme button in the [title bar](workbench-titlebar).
2. To change the colours, open **Settings**, then **Appearance**, and set **Palette**, **Ground**, **Accent** and **Text brightness**.
3. To change how big everything is, press the **Size** button at the right of the [status bar](workbench-status-bar). Pick a preset, or move the interface slider.
4. To change only the text, move the text slider in the same panel.
5. To keep the sizes you chose, press **Save preset…**.
6. To go back, press **Reset**.

The window redraws at once, and Ubiq remembers the choice.

The same controls are in **Settings**, then **Size**, with **Preset**, **Interface size** and **Text size**. **Reset** leaves the nudges for chrome, conversation and content text alone. [Appearance and size](settings-appearance) lists every setting.
