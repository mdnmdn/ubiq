Use this when you want to see more than one pane at once, or put a panel somewhere else.

1. Drag a panel's tab out of its strip.
2. To put it beside another panel as a tab, drop it on the middle of that panel's group.
3. To split, drop it on a group's edge: the edge you pick decides whether a new row or a new column appears.
4. To resize, drag the divider between two groups.
5. To start another pane to put beside the first, press **+** at the end of a pane's tab strip, or its chevron for a hidden pane, other shells and **Logs**.
6. To read one panel at full size, press the zoom button at the strip's far right, and press it again to give the space back.
7. To show or hide a whole region, use the three region buttons in the [title bar](workbench-titlebar).

The panels sit where you dropped them, and Ubiq remembers the arrangement for that mode.

A panel dropped where its kind cannot sit goes back to its own region: the explorer stays in the left or right region and a file stays in the centre. Moving a pane never restarts its agent. [Panels and the dock](workbench-panels) lists what each region accepts.
