Use this when you want a tab out of the way without stopping its agent, or gone for good.

1. Right-click the tab, and pick **Hide** to keep its harness running or **Close** to end it. **Close** asks you to confirm first.
2. To change what the tab's **×** does, open **Settings**, then **Editor**, then **Closing a tab**, and set **Terminal tab**, **Agent terminal tab** or **Agent chat tab** to **Hide** or **Close**.
3. To bring a hidden terminal back, open the chevron beside **+** on a tab strip and pick it from the **Detached** group.
4. To bring back a hidden agent shown as a conversation, click its row in the sidebar of [Agents mode](agents-starting), or open the **Agents not on screen** chevron beside **New agent** in the title bar.

A hidden pane keeps its screen live, and reopening it shows the same screen mid-sentence.

Shells and agent terminals start on **Close**, and chat tabs on **Hide**. **Close** on a conversation also deletes its transcript. A pinned tab has no **×** and no **Hide**; pick **Unpin** first. [Panes](panes) explains why a tab and its harness are separate.
